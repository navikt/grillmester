# Grill the decision

1. State the outcome being clarified and the decisions that appear to block it.
2. Inspect the repository and available tools for facts before asking the user. Separate verified facts, inferences, and user-owned choices.
3. Order the choices by dependency so an answer does not rely on a later unresolved decision.
4. Ask exactly one decision question at a time. Include a recommended answer and its concrete consequence; mention an alternative only when its trade-off is material.
5. Wait for the answer, record what it locks, and follow the affected branch. Revisit an earlier decision only when new evidence invalidates it.
6. Continue until the relevant branches are resolved. Summarize the locked outcome, constraints, non-goals, and any genuinely open blocker.

Do not implement, publish, or otherwise act on the result until the user confirms that shared understanding has been reached.
